## 🤝 **Contributing to Stockie**

Stockie welcomes contributors from all backgrounds - whether you're a developer, financial professional, UX designer, or someone passionate about personal finance technology. Frank's leadership provides a strong foundation, but the platform's success depends on community involvement and diverse perspectives.

### **How to Get Involved**
- **Developers**: Contribute to Python pipelines, C++ services, or Qt UI development
- **Financial Experts**: Help refine algorithms, validate approaches, and ensure regulatory compliance  
- **UX/UI Designers**: Improve user experience and interface design
- **Domain Experts**: Contribute knowledge in areas like tax optimization, retirement planning, or risk management
- **Testers & Users**: Provide feedback, report issues, and suggest improvements

### **Recognition**
All contributors will be acknowledged in this AUTHORS.md file. Significant contributors may be invited to join the Stockie Foundation's technical advisory board.
